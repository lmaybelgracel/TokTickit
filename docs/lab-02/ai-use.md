## My Reflection

AI helped me break a broad stakeholder request into smaller, testable Issues and repeatedly compare the implementation with the engineering contract. The most useful approach was incremental prompting: I asked what would be changed, completed one Issue, ran the relevant tests, and then requested peer review before moving forward.

I also learned not to accept every AI-generated explanation immediately. During the work, some repository status and GitHub-linking details needed to be checked again against the actual files, test output, and Development panel. Moving from Antigravity to Codex also showed the importance of keeping specifications and Git history current so another tool can continue without restarting the project. I remain responsible for the scope, design choices, review responses, evidence, and final submission.
